## Tools

Godot Engine 4.7 (MIT). Node.js is used for the relayer (no npm packages).
NEAR testnet and nearblocks.io are used for settlement and the explorer.

## Monster sprites (`assets/beasts/`)

The sprite beasts are cardboard-ized derivatives (wobbly paper rim, ink outline,
paper grain, flatter colours) of the sprite pack in `assets/monsters/`, made by
`tools/cardboardize.py`. The pack's own licence is not recorded in this repo:
confirm it before a commercial release. The painted-battler and Angela Quidam
sheets are CC BY-NC and are NOT used or shipped. Dropped sprites are listed in
`assets/beasts/MANIFEST.json`. `assets/monsters/` is excluded from exports.
